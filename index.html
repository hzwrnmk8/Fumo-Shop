About Touhou

Touhou Project is a Japanese bullet hell game series created by Jun'ya "ZUN" Ōta. The series began in 1996 and is known for its challenging danmaku gameplay, memorable characters, and distinctive music.

Beyond the games, Touhou has inspired a passionate fan community and countless fan-made creations. Among the most beloved merchandise are Fumo plushies, adorable plush dolls featuring characters from the Touhou universe.

At Fumo Shop, we celebrate the charm of Touhou and its characters by bringing these lovable plushies closer to fans.
