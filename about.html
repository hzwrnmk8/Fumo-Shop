About Touhou Project

The Touhou Project is a Japanese game series known for its memorable characters, music, and bullet-hell gameplay. Its stories mainly take place in Gensokyo, a fictional land where humans and supernatural beings coexist.

This page introduces the Touhou Project, its creator, and the Fumo plushies loved by many fans. More information will be added as the website develops.

Who Created Touhou?

The Touhou Project was created by Jun'ya Ota, better known by his nickname ZUN. He is a game developer and composer responsible for much of the series' music and games.

What Are Fumo Plushies?

"Fumo" is a popular nickname for plush dolls based on characters from the Touhou Project. Fans often collect them, display them, and take photographs of them.

Fumo plushies have become a recognizable part of Touhou fan culture.

The Touhou Community

The Touhou community is known for its fan-made music, illustrations, games, merchandise, and other creative works. Fans from different backgrounds contribute to the series' large and diverse fan culture.
